BEFN / BLACK EYE FIGHT NEWS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Black Eye Fight News (BEFN) is a non-commercial combat sports news and information website and installable web app covering boxing, MMA and grappling. This policy explains how information is handled by the website and associated social-media integrations.

1. Information we process

BEFN does not currently offer public account registration or request visitors to provide their names, email addresses or passwords to browse its news and fight content. It may process the following information:

  • Public sports and social-media content: publicly available posts, captions, public account names and handles, profile images, post identifiers, original post URLs, dates, and publicly supplied engagement metrics obtained from permitted sources or platform APIs, where available.
  • Developer-authorised account information: when a platform account is connected to enable an integration, the relevant platform may provide the authorised account ID, permissions and access credentials. Credentials are stored in restricted deployment configuration rather than public website code or data files.
  • Technical delivery information: the hosting provider and external content providers may process network requests, IP addresses, browser/device data and security or diagnostic logs when visitors use the site.
  • Communications: information that a person chooses to provide when submitting an enquiry, copyright concern or deletion request.

2. Why we use this information

BEFN uses source information to display original, attributed combat-sports coverage; link readers to original posts and publishers; verify the provenance of athlete, promoter and media accounts; refresh news, results and post feeds; and maintain the security and reliability of the service. BEFN does not create social-media posts on another person's behalf through these integrations.

3. Social-media APIs and third parties

Content may be obtained or displayed through X, Instagram, Threads, Facebook, Reddit and Bluesky when access is permitted. Some services are not continuously connected. The site also links to third-party publishers and may load official platform embeds, externally hosted images, web fonts and other content. Opening links or loading embeds can result in the relevant third party receiving technical information and applying its own terms, cookies and privacy policy. BEFN does not control third-party websites.

4. Cookies, caching and analytics

BEFN may use normal browser caching and a service worker to support site performance and installation as a web app. Third-party embedded content and hosting services may use their own cookies or technical identifiers. BEFN does not currently operate a separate account-based advertising or profiling service. Any future analytics or material changes in tracking practices will be reflected in this policy before use.

5. Disclosure and security

Publicly sourced news and post metadata may be published on BEFN. Processing may also involve the site's hosting, code automation and approved social-platform providers. API tokens are not intentionally published in the public repository or exposed in the website's browser source. We do not sell visitors' personal information. No service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

Live Fight Talk history is generally maintained in a rolling four-week window, while selected attributed posts, source registers, editorial records, technical logs or backups may be kept for longer as reasonably needed to operate and document the service. Retention by social platforms, browsers and hosting providers is governed by their own policies. Access tokens are removed when no longer needed, revoked or deleted in response to an applicable request.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may request that BEFN review, correct or remove information it controls, including a public-post reference or connected-account data, using the data deletion instructions. Please identify the relevant post URL, profile or other data so we can locate it. Removing information from BEFN does not delete the original post from a third-party platform, which must be contacted separately.

8. Children

BEFN provides general combat-sports information and is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe information about a child has been processed improperly, please use the deletion request procedure.

9. Contact and updates

For questions or privacy requests, use the BEFN project contact channel and request private handling where appropriate. Do not put sensitive personal information in a public GitHub issue. For direct removal requests, consult data-deletion instructions. We may update this policy as practices change and will publish a new update date here.

Note: This policy describes the current non-commercial website and connected developer tools. Access to third-party platform data is subject to the applicable API permissions and service terms.
