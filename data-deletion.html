Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 11 November 2026

You can ask Black Eye Fight News to remove information it controls, including a source-linked social-media post, account mapping or connected-account data.

1. Submit a request

Open the BEFN project contact channel and select a privacy or data-deletion request in your message. Include the BEFN page or original post URL and a brief description of what you want deleted. Do not post private contact details, access tokens, identity documents or other sensitive information in a public issue. If the request involves sensitive information, ask the administrator to arrange a private way to verify and process it.

2. Review and confirmation

We will review whether the specified information is held or controlled by BEFN. We may need to verify your authority to request deletion before removing another person's data. Once a request is verified, we will remove applicable information from BEFN's active publication and controlled data stores as soon as reasonably practicable, subject to applicable legal or security obligations. We will confirm the result through an appropriate channel.

3. Connected Meta accounts

If you authorised a Meta, Instagram or Threads connection to BEFN, you can also revoke that authorisation from the account's Meta settings. Revocation stops future authorised API access; you may separately request deletion of any retained connected-account details or associated tokens using the process above.

4. Data we do not control

BEFN cannot delete content from Instagram, Threads, Facebook, X, Reddit, Bluesky or other original platforms. Please contact the platform itself to remove the original posts or any information it maintains.

Read the BEFN Privacy Policy for additional details.
