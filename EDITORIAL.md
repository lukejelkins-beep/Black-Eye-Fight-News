# Public website — editorial and publishing rules

## Editorial integrity
- Prefer credible primary fight sources, official event/promoter details and verified fight-record providers. Link original evidence and distinguish confirmed information from unverified reports.
- Do not invent fight bookings, results, fighter records, images, social quotes, dates, handles, video links or audience metrics.
- Give event dates in venue-local time and Melbourne-local start times only when exact local dates/times are confirmed. Keep Australia/AEDT broadcast availability grounded.

## News and results
- Results, scorecards, live-round details, cards and routine results roundups belong in the Results area, grouped by event and by main card/prelims.
- News should cover substantive developments beyond results: title consequences, notable controversies, injuries, unusual finishes, cancellations and meaningful reactions.
- Avoid duplicating the same basic event outcome across Results and Latest News.

## Fight Talk
- Display actual original posts and replies, with precise attribution and permanent source links, not fabricated mock posts or paraphrases presented as quotations.
- Balance champions, contenders, prospects, boxers, MMA fighters, grapplers, coaches, promoters and genuine fan discussion. Minimise repeated syndicated media accounts.
- Mix platforms where accessible (Instagram, Threads, X, Facebook, Reddit, Bluesky), correctly indicating when only a linked source rather than a verified API embed is available.
- Keep Live & relevant focused on recent posts and Highlights on the past 28 days. Never pad those sections with undated or unrelated older posts.
- Ensure athlete photographs and social author avatars are accurate and fit their frames without cropping faces.

## Calendars, champions and scouting
- Update upcoming confirmed events and undercard details using promoters, Tapology, BoxRec, UFC and similarly credible sources.
- Keep wrestling and grappling coverage, including Real American Freestyle (RAF), with relevant calendars.
- Use readable champion name-based photo previews, verified records, and honest placeholders when information is unavailable.

## Publishing boundary
This repository and its Render deployment are intended only for the public live website. Do not commit private editorial publications, dated standalone reports, internal subscriber lists, private contact details or email-only materials. Related private work may use the same factual sources and visual conventions without becoming part of the public website.
