About BEFN

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Black Eye Fight News (BEFN) is an independent, non-commercial combat sports site based in Melbourne, Australia. It covers boxing, MMA and sumo: fight cards, results, champions, fighter profiles, fight film and what fighters and promoters are saying.

Independence

BEFN is not affiliated with the UFC, PFL, ONE Championship, any boxing promoter, any sanctioning body (WBA, WBC, IBF, WBO) or any broadcaster. Nobody pays to appear on BEFN, and coverage choices are BEFN's own.

How we source and check

  • Every fact has a source. Results, records, title reigns and fight dates come from promoters, sanctioning bodies and official statistics. Each is checked against its source before it is shown.
  • If it can't be checked, it isn't shown. A record, rank or result that can't be tied to a source is left out rather than guessed. A fighter page shows only what BEFN can stand behind.
  • Reported, not confirmed, says so. Fights that are reported but not yet officially confirmed are labelled that way.
  • Original posts, not summaries. Fight Talk shows fighters' and promoters' own posts and links to the original on each platform.
  • Fight film is the publisher's own. Every clip is the promoter's or broadcaster's upload, played through their official player or opened on their site.

Photographs

BEFN uses fighter photographs only from sources it is permitted to use: promoters' press and media material, and openly licensed images. Every photograph is credited and listed with its source and licence on the boxing, MMA and sumo credits pages. BEFN does not use news-agency photographs, and it never copies images from sites that do not allow it.

Corrections and removals

If something on BEFN is wrong, or you hold the rights to an image and want it credited differently or removed, tell us and we will fix or remove it. Removal requests for personal information follow the data deletion instructions.

Contact

For corrections, rights questions, media or promoter enquiries, use the BEFN contact channel. Please don't put private details in a public message; say you'd like a private reply and we'll follow up.
