Sumo photograph credits

Official JSA portraits: The approved portrait manifest includes official Japan Sumo Association photographs, with permission attested by the BEFN operator on 16 November 2026. These are not claimed to be Creative Commons licensed. Original source URLs and wrestler identities are retained in the approved photo manifest. CSS may crop photographs for display, and on matchup cards it lightens and blends the studio backgrounds into the page; the image files themselves are not edited.
