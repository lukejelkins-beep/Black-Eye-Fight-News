# Design review: clicks and space (28 Nov 2026)

Owner asked for a broad review: "it takes too many clicks to get where I need to go in some cases", "poor use of space in some places", sortable champion and top 15 columns, more on one page, rating tables on each division page, and the Analysis and Fighters hub areas complementing each other. Checked in Chromium at 390 px and 1280 px against `main` at `2d1d8c4`.

## Done in this change (PR on `claude/serene-hopper-hpk61y`)
- **Sortable champion and top 15.** Each division page now has one table, "Champion and top 15" (the champion is a gold "C" row). Desktop: tap any column heading (Rank, Fighter, Stage, Form, Overall, Form rank); tap again to reverse. Phone: the same sorts as a chip row. Fighters without a value (uncertified Form, no form rank) always sit at the bottom.
- **Division leaderboards on every division page.** Top 5 in the division for BEFN rating, Form, Striking, Grappling, Wrestling and Results, with the official rank beside each name, "All N ›" to the full table, and one link to that division's striking, grappling and defence boards on Analysis.
- **Analysis leaderboards: "Top 10s" view (default).** Every board as a compact top-10 card, grouped Ratings, Striking, Grappling, Defence, Finishing and pace. The window (last 5, 12 months, 36 months, career) applies to every stat card. "All N ›" on a card opens the existing full table for that board in place; "Full table" is the old single-board view. Division gains "All men" and "All women".
- **The two areas link both ways.** Fighters hub top bar: "Leaderboards ›" opens Analysis on the division (or men/women) being viewed and scrolls to the boards. Analysis: an "On this page" row under the title jumps to Stats leaderboards, Champions or Title fights, plus "Fighters hub ›".
- **Denser layout.** Desktop top-15 rows 68 → 52 px with last five beside the name; champion card 300 → 220 px; Coming through photos smaller; on wide screens (1200 px+) the podium sits beside Rank vs form instead of above it. Phone rows 64 → 54 px. The MMA Fighters hub Overall page is about 13% shorter at 1280 px.

## Clicks: what was too deep (proposals at the time)
1. **Analysis on a phone puts the leaderboards about 2,000 px down**, under the champion carousel (one champion per screen) and How fights ended. The new jump row helps; the bigger fix is to make the champions a compact strip (11 small portraits with name and division, tap for the detail) so the leaderboards start within two screens.
2. **Find a fighter goes to another page.** The hub's "Find a fighter" opens Analysis and the user then scrolls to a search box. Proposal: a search field at the top of the Fighters hub itself that lists matches as you type (the data is already loaded on that page).
3. **A division is two taps from anywhere else** (Fighters, then the division pill). Proposal: division names on fighter profiles, results and the Analysis cards link straight to `#fighters` on that division.
4. **Fight predictions is a link row, then a page.** Fine as is; worth showing the next card's three highest-confidence picks inline on Analysis so the common question is answered without the extra tap.

## Space: what was wasteful (proposals at the time)
1. **Fighters page with "All sports" (boxing grid):** 203 cards, most with a 200 px "Photo unavailable" initials tile. Proposal: when there is no photo, use the compact row layout (name, record, division, one line) instead of a tall placeholder card; about four times as many fighters per screen.
2. **Division pulse (hub Overall):** tiles are 230-300 px tall with eight divisions, so the last one sits alone on a row at 1280 px. Left unchanged here because the owner asked on 25 Nov for the pulse photos to be as prominent as possible; the option is equal tiles with no double-width top tile (two rows of four) and 150 px photos.
3. **Percentile rating boards tie at the top.** Results, Striking, Grappling and Wrestling are whole percentiles, so the top 10 is often ten fighters at "99th", all ranked 1. The cards show it honestly; a finer value (one decimal, or the underlying rating) in `data/ufc-stats/leaderboards.json` would separate them. That is a stats-build change and needs the owner.
4. **Analysis "How fights ended"** spends a full column on four bars; it could sit beside Title fights coming up as a single strip.
