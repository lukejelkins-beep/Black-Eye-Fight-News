# Real-phone and Safari checklist: Sumo, 28 Nov 2026

For the Sumo work in #575, #581, #583 and the Bouts-page chances. I can only test in Chromium, so this list covers what a phone and Safari can do differently: touch, swiping, the share sheet, the native dropdowns, and the chart's tap targets.

Open https://blackeyefightnews.com.au/sumo.html on an iPhone in Safari. If the site is installed as an app, pull to refresh twice so the new version loads. Each line is a yes/no check. For any "no", send a screenshot and say which phone and iOS version.

## Navigation
- [ ] Sumo, then Analysis: the five buttons (Basho review, Insights, Stories, Predictor, Techniques) sit in two rows. None is cut off at the right edge.
- [ ] Sumo, then Fighters (Rikishi, Rankings, Changes, Stables): all four are visible, in one or two rows.
- [ ] Open a wrestler's profile: his name is visible without scrolling.

## Sumo home
- [ ] Under the hero, "The basho in brief" shows four cards: Champions and prizes, Gold stars, Sanyaku scorecard, On the move. Each has a row of small faces.
- [ ] Tap "Gold stars": the Basho review opens and scrolls to Gold stars once it has loaded (a moment's pause is fine).
- [ ] Back button: you return to the Sumo home.

## Basho review (Analysis, then Basho review)
- [ ] The title-race chart shows four coloured lines, with the names and records above it.
- [ ] Tap the chart near day 8: the day view below changes to Day 8 and the page does not jump.
- [ ] Day buttons: one row you can swipe sideways, with the chosen day (15) in view. Swipe to day 3 and tap it: Day 3 shows; the strip stays where you left it.
- [ ] Tap "Juryo": Juryo results and standings show. Tap "Makuuchi": back to Makuuchi.
- [ ] Copy the page link after choosing Juryo and day 3, then paste it in a new tab: the same day and division open.
- [ ] "Who did well, who didn't": swipe the photo tiles sideways. They snap into place and nothing is stretched.
- [ ] Tournament dropdown: Safari's own picker opens. Choosing "July 2025" loads that basho.
- [ ] After-day recap: tap "Share". The iPhone share sheet opens with the recap text. Cancel. Tap "Copy": "Copied" appears.

## Live basho preview
- [ ] Open `sumo.html#review?liveday=9`. The yellow "Preview" label shows, then "Day 10 picks", the race chart and the day view.

## Predictor (Analysis, then Predictor)
- [ ] Both dropdowns open Safari's picker. Choose Hoshoryu, then Kotozakura: the faces, percentages and "What decides it" update and the page does not jump to the top.
- [ ] The swap button (⇄) swaps the two wrestlers.
- [ ] "What decides it" shows seven rows. The bars point left (blue) or right (red), and no text overlaps a bar.
- [ ] "Live record" says it starts with the January 2027 basho.
- [ ] "How the predictor works" opens and closes.

## Bouts (Fights, then Bouts)
- [ ] Under each Makuuchi bout there is a beige strip, "Predictor that morning: … %", with Called, Missed or Upset at the right.
- [ ] Tap a strip: the Predictor opens with those two wrestlers.
- [ ] Switch to Juryo: most bouts have the strip. Switch to Makushita: no strip, which is expected.

## Stories (Analysis, then Stories)
- [ ] The page opens with a dark band titled "True, surprising and sourced". Headings are not in capitals.
- [ ] Every story card has a blue photo band at the top. Within a row, the cards' bottoms line up.
- [ ] Rows swipe sideways. "See all" expands a row into a grid, and "Show fewer" collapses it.
- [ ] Long descriptions stop after six lines with "…" instead of spilling.

## Insights
- [ ] "Underrated and overrated" shows photo tiles. Names are not cut off; a long name may wrap to two lines.

## Desktop Safari (Mac), if you have one
- [ ] The same pages at full width: the title-race chart has names at the line ends, and the day view shows results and standings side by side.
