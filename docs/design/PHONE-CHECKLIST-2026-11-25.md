# Real-phone checklist: changes shipped 24–25 Nov 2026

Open https://blackeyefightnews.com.au on your phone. If the site is installed as an app, pull to refresh twice so the new version loads. Each line is a yes/no check; note anything that's a "no" and send a screenshot.

## Navigation (phone option B)
- [ ] The slim top bar shows BEFN, Boxing / MMA / Sumo and a menu icon.
- [ ] Scroll down: the top bar slides away. Scroll up a little: it comes back.
- [ ] The bottom tabs (Overview, Fights, Fighters, Analysis, Fight Talk) stay under your thumb, and the active one is in the sport's colour.

## Front page and Overviews
- [ ] The front page hero changes sport as it rotates.
- [ ] Boxing and MMA Overviews: upcoming fights show posters where one exists.

## Fights
- [ ] Upcoming: posters first; photo cards for fights without a poster.
- [ ] Results: faces, winner in bold, method and round; tap a result for the full card.
- [ ] Fight film: one big clip on top, then "Latest finishes", then the filter chips and a 2-column grid.
- [ ] Tap a YouTube clip: it plays inside the card, with no jump to the YouTube app. Rotate to full screen and back.
- [ ] No Shorts (vertical videos) appear on Fight film. The collector marks them on its next run, so give it up to an hour after deploy.

## Fighter profiles (new design)
- [ ] Open a UFC fighter (for example Ilia Topuria from MMA → Fighters). The hero shows the photo, record, pills, next fight, and the rating with last-5 form on its own row.
- [ ] Tabs (Overview / Fights / Stats / Compare / News) are reachable with your thumb; swipe the tab row sideways if it's cut off.
- [ ] Open a boxer and a PFL or ONE fighter: no "Not rated", "No … listed" or "Unassessed" anywhere.
- [ ] "About this profile" at the bottom opens and closes.
- [ ] Paste a profile link into a new tab (for example `/#fighter/ufc--ilia-topuria`): the profile opens over the Fighters page, not a page stuck on "Loading the latest fights…".
- [ ] Close the profile: you land on a working page.

## Analysis: champions carousel
- [ ] MMA → Analysis: the carousel starts at Heavyweight. Swipe left: Light heavyweight, and the counter, chip and dot all move with it.
- [ ] Tap a division chip: it jumps to that champion.
- [ ] The cut-out fades into the page with no hard edges, and the details below are readable.
- [ ] Boxing → Analysis: the same carousel above "The title picture"; each slide lists every belt holder.
- [ ] "Latest stoppages" shows a video thumbnail where one exists, and tapping it plays in the page.

## Fight Talk
- [ ] Cards show the face, name, role, text and likes; "Hottest right now" is on top.
- [ ] "Show post" loads the real post in place.
- [ ] The Fighters / Everyone switch and the platform chips work; "More filters" opens.

## About and credits
- [ ] The footer has "About BEFN"; the page opens and the email link opens your mail app. (Live after #547 merges.)
- [ ] The MMA and boxing photo credits pages open from the footer.

## Everywhere
- [ ] No sideways scrolling on any page.
- [ ] Nothing is too small to read or too small to tap.
- [ ] Dark top bar, light pages; nothing flashes white or black while loading.
