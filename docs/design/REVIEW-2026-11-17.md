# Site review — 2026-11-17

Method: the live production site (`black-eye-fight-news.onrender.com`) viewed in a real browser at 1440 px (desktop) and 375 px (phone), pages News, Fighter scout and Fights, plus a read of the repository and its data. The Sumo preview was reviewed separately and is already being rebuilt to the new direction. Not reviewed in depth: Champions, UFC stats, The conversation, Fight film, Results (reviewed structurally only).

## What is working
- **Depth no competitor of this size has:** 783-fighter UFC cohort, round-by-round stats, results, a This Week fight list, news summaries.
- **The phone layout already behaves like an app:** bottom tab bar (News, Film, Fights, Results, Fight Talk), large readable headlines, no horizontal scroll.
- **Fighter scout cards are the right idea:** photo, weight class, record, one line of analysis.
- **A recognisable palette:** red, gold, cream and near-black.

## What makes it feel basic or dated
1. **The header spends the first screen on the logo.** The logo is a raster image with a black box behind it, sitting in a much larger black band: about 170 px of height on desktop and phone before any content, with most of the band empty.
2. **Three navigation systems at once.** Seven top tabs, then a sport filter row, then in-page tabs. Labels repeat (News twice, Fights twice). The three sport buttons are icon-only and their meaning is unclear.
3. **Dated headline treatment.** Section titles ("Top stories", "Fighter profiles") are chrome/bevel italic images. Five type families are in use (Russo One, DM Sans, Georgia, system-ui, plus the image titles). It reads as mid-2010s rather than modern.
4. **Staff tools lead the Fighters page.** First screen of content is an "Archive verification note", then a "Scouting intelligence · review queue" with "MEDIUM REVIEW PRIORITY" labels, then "Round-by-round intelligence". The actual fighter cards are two scrolls down. This is research output, not reader content.
5. **Placeholder text is visible.** Fight listings show "Start time TBC · venue date" literally.
6. **Dense strings instead of visuals.** Round stats are lines of small text ("Significant strikes 5–15 / Takedowns 1–0 / Control 19s / 7s"). Bars and big numbers would be faster to read.
7. **Photography is uneven.** Different crops, backgrounds and quality from card to card; news images are letterboxed with side bars; one news card showed a broken-image icon at review time. See IMAGE-GUIDE.md: none of 884 fighter photos is hosted by BEFN.
8. **Events have no poster treatment.** The This Week list shows a tiny fighter thumbnail, not event art, although `data/historical-posters.json` shows poster sourcing already exists for past events.
9. **Two visual languages.** The Sumo preview (UFC-style, consistent headers) and the main site now look like different products.
10. **Weight.** Large inline scripts were already moved out of `index.html`; the published root still ships very large research data (the repo's `data/` is about 107 MB, of which `ufc-research-archive.json` alone is 64 MB). A Render publish-path change would keep it off the public site.

## Recommended direction
See DIRECTION.md. In one line: dark editorial surfaces, big consistent photography, uppercase condensed headlines, one accent, one header and nav pattern, reader content only.

## Roadmap (in order)
Tick items as they ship.

**Phase 0 — foundations (small, enables everything)**
- [ ] One shared stylesheet of tokens (colour, type, spacing, radius) used by main and Sumo.
- [ ] Vector or transparent logo; compact header (about 56 px).
- [ ] Image register and processing script (IMAGE-GUIDE.md); migrate the top headliners first.
- [ ] Remove "venue date" and other placeholder strings; fix broken-image fallbacks.

**Phase 1 — front door**
- [ ] One primary navigation (four to five items), same on phone and desktop; labelled sport filter.
- [ ] Home: full-bleed lead story, then a tight "This week" strip with posters, then latest news.
- [ ] Replace image headings with live text headings.

**Phase 2 — fighters and events**
- [ ] Fighters: card grid first; scouting and review queue moved to a staff-only view.
- [ ] Athlete profile in the UFC-style hero-band pattern (proven on Sumo).
- [ ] Event pages with poster, main card, local start times, results when done.
- [ ] Round stats as bars and big numbers.

**Phase 3 — polish and speed**
- [ ] Self-hosted, resized WebP/AVIF everywhere; lazy loading; a performance budget per page.
- [ ] Publish-path change so research data is not shipped to readers (owner action in Render).
- [ ] Accessibility pass: contrast, tap targets, alt text.

## Sumo (pilot) status
Done: four-item navigation, one page header, live refresh every 30 minutes with auto-deploy, career and head-to-head kept current, automatic handling of new basho and new wrestlers. Next: compact result and matchup rows, profile hero band, rankings polish, then speed.
