# BEFN — request to license official rikishi headshots

**Status:** Draft only. Not sent. No permission has been granted.

**To:** Japan Sumo Association, Public Relations / licensing contact (address to be verified before sending)

**Subject:** Request for permission to display official rikishi profile photographs — Black Eye Fight News (Australia)

Hello,

I operate Black Eye Fight News (BEFN), an independent Australian combat-sports information website. We are building a non-production Sumo information section with profiles and banzuke records for 190 currently ranked rikishi: 42 Makuuchi, 28 Juryo, and 120 Makushita.

We would like permission to reproduce the official JSA profile headshot for each of these wrestlers on their individual profile and in small roster/rankings cards. Images would be used only to identify the wrestler in editorial sports coverage. We would provide any required credit, copyright notice, link to the JSA profile, and reasonable image protection measures. We will not represent BEFN as an official JSA partner.

Could you please advise:
1. Whether a licence is available for editorial website use of the 190 official profile headshots;
2. Any licence fee, geographic/term restrictions, attribution or image-modification requirements;
3. Whether you can supply a roster-matched media package or approved image URLs/files; and
4. Whether permission covers use in BEFN's responsive web and mobile/PWA presentation.

We can provide the full roster, sample card designs and usage details for review. No JSA-owned images will be published until written permission or a compatible licence has been confirmed.

Thank you for your guidance.

Black Eye Fight News
https://blackeyefightnews.com.au
admin@blackeyefightnews.com.au

## Image collection policy
- The canonical roster mapping is `data/sumo/official-portrait-research.json`.
- Official profile links are *source candidates*, not confirmed photo downloads or permission.
- Existing independently licensed photographs remain available in `data/sumo/approved-photos.json`.
- Before publishing any additional image, verify wrestler identity, photographer/rightsholder, licence/permission scope, attribution, source URL and image integrity.
- No hotlinking or bulk scraping of JSA image files without permission.
- The first priority is the 42 Makuuchi wrestlers, then 28 Juryo, then 120 Makushita.
