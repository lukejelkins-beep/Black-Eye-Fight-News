BEFN Sumo · Proposal for the owner · 21 Nov 2026

From a facts page to facts everywhere

A rethink of Sumo Fun Facts. One fact engine feeds short, surprising, sourced facts into every part of the sumo site: matchups, head-to-heads, profiles, stables and techniques. The Fun Facts page becomes the hub where they're all collected.

Change the unitBuild single facts, each about specific wrestlers, a pair or a technique, instead of whole pages of cards.
Change the rulesEvery fact passes two checks: is it true, and is it surprising enough to show?
Change the placementShow a fact beside the bout a reader is already looking at.

Where we are

Why the current page doesn't wow

I reviewed the pilot as a fan would see it, on a 390 px phone and a 1280 px desktop. The data is mostly right now. The problem is what we choose to show and where we show it.

It repeats the results table

"X is unbeaten", "X has won five straight", "X is one win from kachi-koshi". A fan watching the basho already knows these, and most of the page's 30 cards are this kind.

Too long, too samey

About 10,200 px of scrolling on a phone, with the same card shape repeated. Four stat counters open the page ("190 tracked rikishi"), which is internal bookkeeping rather than a story.

Cut off from the rest of the site

Facts only live on their own page. Fans spend their time on matchups, head-to-heads and profiles, which is exactly where a fact would land.

The rules choose safe over interesting

Only Sumo API numbers or six hand-written stories can appear. There's no way to use outside sources at scale, and no way to say "this is surprising enough to show".

Our best material goes unused

We store about 58,000 bouts with day, winner and technique. That holds rivalries, technique signatures and final-day pressure records. The page uses almost none of it.

Win % sits in the wrong place

A 30-option leaderboard is a stats tool, not a story. It belongs in Leaderboards, with a few preset windows.

The idea

One fact engine, many places to show facts

Every fact is a small record: what it says, who it's about, where it can appear, its source and how strong that source is, how surprising it is, and when it expires. Pages ask the engine for "the best fact about these two wrestlers" and show the top one.

Decision rules

Two checks: true, then interesting

The pilot's rules only asked "is this safe to show?". The new rules first ask "can we stand behind this exact wording?", then "is it worth a fan's attention here?". Outside sources are allowed, with the check scaled to the type of claim.

A · Official

JSA pages and official results.

  • Publish automatically when read by a tested script
  • Allowed for titles, prizes, kinboshi, debut, origin, career totals
B · Our numbers

Calculated from BEFN's stored data.

  • Publish automatically if the calculation is tested and the sample is complete
  • The scope must be in the wording ("in BEFN's records since 2021")
  • No unscoped "most ever" or "first ever"
  • New kinds of fact get a one-off check against JSA before they go live
C · Researched

Media, Wikipedia, Guinness, NHK, interviews.

  • Two independent sources, or one strong one plus owner approval
  • Saved with link, quoted line and date checked
  • Re-checked every 180 days (biography) or 90 days (records)
D · Never

Doesn't run, whatever the source.

  • Match-fixing hints or implied bias
  • Injury guesses, private life, rumours
  • A record claim wider than its source

Wow score: what decides whether a true fact gets shown

RarityHow unusual compared with everyone else (top 5% or better scores high)
StakesInvolves today's bout, the title race, a promotion or a sanyaku wrestler
RecencyHappening now or recently beats something from 2014
SpecificityA clear number and named people, said in one sentence
FreshnessNot shown to this reader recently, and not something the standings already show

Each place on the site has its own bar. A matchup line needs a high score, because it's a single line; the hub can go deeper. If nothing scores high enough, nothing shows. Where facts tie, rotate them.

Open with one fact

Lead with a single fact of the day in a large, two-wrestler layout. The counters go.

Themed rows, not one long feed

Rivalries, Rising stars, Technique, Pressure, Giant-killers, Families and roots, Records. Each row shows 4 to 6 cards and a "See all".

Search by wrestler or pair

Type a name, or two names, and get that wrestler's or that pair's best facts. This shares code with the head-to-head page.

Live form only during a basho

A small "This basho" row (title race, unbeaten, chasing 7–0) appears only during a tournament, so the page stays interesting between basho.

Win % moves to Leaderboards

Preset windows (this basho, last 3, last 6, last 12, two years). Wrestlers who missed a basho are shown with a "missed basho" label rather than hidden.

Plan

Four phases, each a preview PR

Phase 1 · recommended start

Engine and matchups

The fact store, truth checks and wow score. Rivalry, streak, technique and size facts. One fact line on today's matchups and the head-to-head panel. This is where fans will notice most for the least work.

Phase 2

Profiles, kimarite, stables

Did-you-know facts and badges on profiles, "who wins with this" in the kimarite guide, stable facts. Pressure and comeback facts after their audit.

Phase 3

Stories hub

Rebuild Fun Facts as Sumo Stories with fact of the day, themed rows and pair search. Move win % to Leaderboards.

Phase 4

Outside research

The JSA profile reader (titles, prizes, kinboshi, career totals), weekly research PRs and the pre-basho storyline pack.
