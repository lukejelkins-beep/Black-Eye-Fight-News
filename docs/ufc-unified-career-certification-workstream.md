## Strict per-fighter decision
1. Reuse existing saved UFCalendar fight IDs, paired corners, results, dates, and measured statistics. Check independently sourced full UFC.com fighter histories and pinned Greco UFCStats-derived complete source links, from debut through 14 Nov 2026.
2. Verify no missing or extra completed official UFC bouts, exact original numeric ID and both corners, W/L/draw/NC, real event dates (documented UTC ±1 day where appropriate), and all required round observations. Do not match based solely on fighter names or a self-declared total.
3. Exclude independently identified DWCS, non-UFC RTU qualifiers and TUF exhibitions; include actual official UFC/TUF finals, UFC-branded eligible showcases and official results even when a card title is misleading. Maintain a reviewed exception rather than overriding an uncertain bout.
4. **Stage 3 audit must pass for each candidate** to move from evidence-only to main-certified. Leave each failed/ambiguous candidate in fastLane/exception with exact reason and latest attempted source; retry distinct fighters instead of repeating exhausted provider searches.
5. Preserve a single read-only 783-person audit and publish only its successful CI artifact for status. A running or failed Actions job, an attestation document, or V4's distinct 147 count does not independently pass the main gate.
