## Credential fields and UFC outcomes

For each claim record fighter slug, discipline, style, organizer, event, date, opponent where relevant, country or jurisdiction, age class, division, result, evidence URL, and review status. Keep competition level (regional, national, continental, world, Olympic) separate from result (selection, start, placement, title). Use distinct credential types for promotion titles, tournament titles, stadium belts and UFC entry routes. Preserve youth, senior and masters distinctions. Record source coverage and identity conflicts per fighter. An unresolved record stays unknown.

Start with senior world-level freestyle and Greco wrestlers, split by participation and medal. Count only credentials dated before UFC debut. For a first-five-bout comparison include UFC main-roster bouts from debut, handle draws and no contests explicitly, and show fighters who have yet to reach five bouts. Compare with other fighters from similar debut eras, divisions and ages. Report fighter and bout denominators, uncertainty, and coverage gaps. Add descriptive career win rate and time to first UFC title bout with follow-up duration shown. Let fighters qualify in multiple disciplines, and distinguish the screened comparison group from fighters whose credentials remain unknown. Interpret any differences as observational associations.
