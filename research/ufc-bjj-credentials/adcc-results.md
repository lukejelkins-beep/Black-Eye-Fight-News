Six fighters are confirmed divisional champions and seven earned a medal. One additional cohort fighter, Chris Weidman, has documented Worlds bouts and a separate Trials title.

## Bracket and Trials evidence

- `chris-weidman` won the middleweight division of the 2009 USA East Coast Trials. At Worlds he defeated Dan Tabera and lost to André Galvão at -88 kg. In absolute he defeated Antoine Jaoude and lost in the quarterfinal to Vinny Magalhães. [Trials report](https://adcombat.com/news2009-06-13adcc-usa-east-coast-trials-champions/) · [Worlds day one](https://adcombat.com/news2009-09-26adcc-2009-live-results/) · [Worlds day two](https://adcombat.com/news2009-09-27adcc-2009-live-results/) · [UFC identity corroboration](https://www.ufc.com/node/72006)
- `kron-gracie` competed at 2009 Worlds, defeating Enrico Cocco before losing to Marcelo Garcia. `rani-yahya` defeated Kouhei Yasumi and Jeff Glover before withdrawing injured. `gunnar-nelson` also competed in the weight bracket; James Brasco defeated him there. [Day one](https://adcombat.com/news2009-09-26adcc-2009-live-results/) · [Day two](https://adcombat.com/news2009-09-27adcc-2009-live-results/)

- `gilbert-burns` was listed as withdrawn from the 2017 men’s -77 kg bracket in the organizer’s late roster update. [ADCC withdrawal announcement](https://adcombat.com/justin-rader-and-luiz-panza-out-nicky-ryan-sergio-ardila-and-jackson-sousa-all-in/)

## Source and identity limits

The first name join covered all 921 slugs against 380 numbered placements on 12 official pages spanning 1998 through 2017, with punctuation and accents normalized. A follow-up resolved Beatriz Mesquita to Bia Mesquita and added the 64 numbered non-superfight slots on the 2019 and 2022 official pages. Marcus Almeida also appears as Marcus Buchecha Almeida. The 2024 and 2026 official pages display brackets as images, so the numbered federation text screen ends at 2022. The 2024 Bia Mesquita entries were checked in the event broadcaster’s results. No cohort placement outside the listed sources is ruled out. This also leaves most nonplacing brackets, regional trials, withdrawals and opens unscreened. [ADCC Worlds archive](https://adcombat.com/event-category/adcc-worlds/) · [2024 full results and bracket images](https://adcombat.com/2024-adcc-world-championship-day-2-full-results/) · [2026 bracket images](https://adcombat.com/adcc-world-championship-2026-brackets-day-2-full-results/)

The 2026 +99 kg winner Victor Hugo is distinct from the 145 lb UFC roster fighter with the same display name. Several historical ADCC winners, including Davi Ramos, do not have a matched canonical slug in the pinned roster and are excluded from counts. [ADCC statistics](https://adcombat.com/adcc-submission-fighting-world-championship-statistics/) · [UFC Victor Hugo](https://www.ufc.com/athlete/victor-hugo)
