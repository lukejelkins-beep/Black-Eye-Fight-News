# IBJJF World Championship archive screen

Screened all 921 canonical roster names against the official IBJJF podium pages. The archive yielded 46,079 placement rows across 63 events, including 30 gi Worlds, 18 No-Gi Worlds and 15 World Master events. Names were case- and accent-normalized. Ordered name tokens and selected full-name aliases generated 361 raw candidates across 84 roster slugs. [IBJJF results index](https://ibjjf.com/events/results)

The table includes 200 strong candidate rows across 47 fighters, with original names, academies and event divisions for identity review. Columns describe the event and the rank recorded on that date. The table is a source-linked screening result. Individual identification remains provisional where the name or competitive history needs independent corroboration.

Austin Bashi is independently corroborated by his UFC biography and the official 2023 No-Gi Worlds results, which place him first in Adult Brown Light. [UFC athlete profile](https://www.ufc.com/athlete/austin-bashi)

UFC accounts corroborate the identities and distinguish the corresponding strong result records for [Mackenzie Dern](https://www.ufc.com/news/mackenzie-dern-makes-invicta-fc-debut-december), [Marcus Almeida](https://www.ufc.com/index.php/news/marcus-buchecha-has-arrived-ufc-abu-dhabi), [Rodolfo Vieira](https://www.ufc.com/index.php/news/fighters-you-should-know-ufc-258-edition-barber-vieira-grasso), [Jaqueline Amorim](https://jp.ufc.com/news/fighters-rise-ufc-kc-amorim-zhang-onama), and [Tabatha Ricci](https://www.ufc.com/athlete/tabatha-ricci?language_content_entity=en). The federation’s original result determines the specific division and placement. UFC profiles also corroborate the competition histories for [Bia Mesquita](https://www.ufc.com/athlete/bia-mesquita), [Talita Alencar](https://www.ufc.com/athlete/talita-alencar), and [Clayton Carpenter](https://www.ufc.com/athlete/clayton-carpenter). Their individual placements remain keyed to the federation results.

The [UFC biography of Michelle Montague](https://www.ufc.com/athlete/michelle-montague) claims a 2022 blue-belt open-class title. The IBJJF result lists Michelle Yvonne Montague in second in Adult Female Blue Open Class. Record the federation placement and flag the biography discrepancy.

The raw matches include obvious collisions. Bruno da Silva Malfacine is a different athlete from the two rostered Bruno Silvas. Several Victor Hugo entries are distinct people. Marlon Loor Vera appears in a 2010 master division that conflicts with the UFC fighter’s age. The short-name José Aldo row and some other plausible names remain unresolved pending club, full-name and age corroboration.
