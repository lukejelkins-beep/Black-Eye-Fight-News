# IBJJF majors screen (Pans, Europeans, Nationals and continentals)

Screened 25 November 2026, with the owner's IBJJF permission. `ibjjf_screen.py` in the research runner read the official podium pages of 220 championship editions, 1996 to 2026. They are the Pan and Pan No-Gi, European and European No-Gi, Brazilian Nationals (gi and no-gi), American Nationals (gi and no-gi), Asian, and South American (gi and no-gi). The Worlds are in [ibjjf-world-results.md](ibjjf-world-results.md) and were not repeated. [IBJJF results index](https://ibjjf.com/events/results)

**Method.** All 921 roster names and aliases were matched against every podium name. The table below keeps only strong rows: the published name starts with the fighter's first name and ends with their surname, the division is adult or juvenile (no masters, teens or kids), and the fighter would have been at least 15 that year, based on the age on their UFC profile. Of 90 fighters with any full-name match, 36 remain. Ten were ruled out on age: Lucas Rocha, José Henrique, Bruno Silva (Blindado), Diego Lopes, Daniel Marcos, Vinicius Oliveira, Jaqueline Amorim, Victor Hugo, Kauê Fernandes and Ricardo Ramos. The rest failed the name or division filter.

**Identity.** *Corroborated* means the fighter's UFC profile names jiu-jitsu or BJJ, and the academy and era fit their known career. *Provisional* rows need a second link before use. The federation's published row determines the division and placing. As with the Worlds file, this is a source-linked screen, not a certificate.
