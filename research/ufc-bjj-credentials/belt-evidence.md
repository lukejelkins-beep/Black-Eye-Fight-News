# BJJ belt evidence

24 November 2026 · Pinned 921-fighter cohort

## UFC athlete biography screen

The check resolved 920 identity-matched UFC profiles. Of those, 560 stated a BJJ rank and 360 had no explicit statement detected. Xiong Jing Nan's direct UFC path returned search, and her academy supplied a separate belt statement. Across the UFC profiles the colours were 223 black, 137 brown, 133 purple, 69 blue, 4 white. The colour counts overlap where a profile records multiple ranks.

The [fighter ledger](belt-coverage.json) contains one row per pinned slug. This pass looked for explicit belt colours next to Brazilian jiu-jitsu or BJJ and checked the profile page title against the roster name. It also accepted documented display-name and official URL variants, including UFC's Ariane da Silva, Casey O'Neill and Abdul Rakhman Yakhyaev profiles. A URL that returned the site's search page was recorded separately from an athlete profile. The screening looked for adjacent style and rank terms in athlete biographies and Q&A, then spot-checked the source context, multiple-belt cases and references to other martial arts or people.

A listed belt is a claim on an official UFC page, often supplied in athlete Q&A. Treat it as the rank documented by that source and record any dated promotion separately. A valid profile without matching text leaves the belt unresolved. The screen can miss unusual wording, rank disclosures in video or graphics, and promotions announced after an athlete filled out the UFC profile.
