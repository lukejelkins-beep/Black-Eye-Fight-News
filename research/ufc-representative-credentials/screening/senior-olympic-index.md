# Senior Olympic index screening

All 921 cohort rows were compared against the independent all-sports Summer and Winter results index at `ismayc/olympic-moderndive-data@d7f254a486c6a24c9b1680cba1747e5d4eabc1a8`. The unmodified source CSV SHA-256 was `b010d575cf3d3f2e2eb8b73b1a4fb833c0766a56fe66ab7b213dab73af6fdded`. Its 315,094 athlete-event rows derive from Sports-Reference for 1896–2016 and Olympedia for 2018–2026. The public `olympicAthletes` package documents its CC BY 4.0 licence and credits. No source CSV or birth dates were added to the project. [Pinned public source](https://github.com/ismayc/olympic-moderndive-data/tree/d7f254a486c6a24c9b1680cba1747e5d4eabc1a8) · [Package provenance](https://github.com/moderndive/olympicAthletes#provenance)

The search kept 23 Games editions from 1984 to 2026, spanning every sport in the selected index. It normalized case, diacritics and punctuation, retained display names and known profile slugs, and compared reordered full token sets, additional or missing middle-name tokens and bounded one-edit differences on a two-token name with an exact companion token. Approximate Games age and available birth date were only triage evidence. The birth-date source covers 889 of the 921 cohort slugs including recognized aliases.
