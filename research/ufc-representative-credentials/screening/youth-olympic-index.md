Matching normalized case, diacritics and punctuation and compared exact and reordered full name token sets and additional or missing name tokens. A Games-year age of 13 to 19 was used to triage fighters with a recorded birth year. All editions were searched for fighters without a recorded birth year. The screen yielded one verified athlete, seven same-name or partial-name collisions and 913 cases with no candidate under these matching rules. This discovery index cannot establish all appearances under different aliases. It is separate from the senior Olympics screen and individual records.
