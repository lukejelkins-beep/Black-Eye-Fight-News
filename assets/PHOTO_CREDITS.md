## Other catalogue entries

The source catalogue at `assets/fighter-portrait-registry.json` contains historical third-party image links (existing in the original research), **not licences**. Do not download, mirror or republish those originals as local copies until reuse rights and correct fighter identity have been checked individually. Most do not have local files.

For entries without an approved photo, the presentation can attempt a public encyclopaedia image lookup and otherwise displays initials. An automated lookup is *not* an identity or licence certification. This remains a pre-launch archived research site; editorial review is required before broader publication.
