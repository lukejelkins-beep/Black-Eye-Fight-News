Do UFC gyms decide fights?

We checked 1,084 UFC fights from 2025 and 2026. Allowing for who each fighter was going in, most gyms look much the same. One does not.

Black Eye Fight News analysis, 28 Nov 2026

The short answer
  • Most gyms finish within a win or two of what their fighters' records predicted, and the big one-season swings tend to reverse the next season.
  • Shuffling fighters between gyms at random gave gym-to-gym differences as large as the real ones 14% of the time. That is a hint that some gyms differ, not proof.
  • The exception is City Kickboxing: 30-11, about 10 wins more than expected, and ahead of expectation in both seasons.

The one that stood out

City Kickboxing won 30 of 41: 14-4 in 2025 (+5.0 against expected) and 16-7 in 2026 (+4.5). Being the best in two separate seasons counts for more than one good year. Even so, with 33 gyms compared, a result this strong would turn up by luck about once in 14 tries, so it is suggestive, not settled.

It also cannot tell us why. A gym that coaches well and a gym that signs fighters on the way up look the same in the results.

The swings that reversed

Xtreme Couture was +3.5 wins against expected in 2025 and −1.3 in 2026. MMA Lab went from −2.5 to +3.3. American Top Team, the busiest gym, went 50-53 over both seasons, about 4 wins under expectation. Turnarounds like these are what luck looks like. Among the smaller gyms (under 20 bouts, not on the chart), the furthest below expectation over both seasons were Galpao da Luta 3-10, MMA Masters 4-12, Fight Ready 5-13. Those are small samples.

Why gyms still matter to fans

This does not say training camps are pointless. It says that a gym's win-loss record mostly reflects the fighters it already has, apart from the odd gym that keeps beating expectation. A great gym that signs great fighters will win a lot, and the record alone cannot tell you which came first.

How we did it

We took every decided UFC bout in 2025 (544 fights, 48 events) and in 2026 up to 21 Nov (540 fights, 44 events), including Road to UFC. For each fighter we worked out their record going into the fight and, from the gap between the two, how likely each was to win. That method picked the winner in 59.6% of bouts. A gym's expected wins are the sum of those chances.

Limits: gyms are where Sherdog lists a fighter training now, not necessarily at the time of the fight, and a fighter who lists several gyms counts for each. Fighters who win may also move to better-known gyms. Expectations come from records, not betting odds. Two seasons rule out big gym effects, not small ones.
